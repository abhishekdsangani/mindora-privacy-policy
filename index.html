1. Overview

MINDORA: Brain Games & Puzzles ("MINDORA", "the app", "we", "us", or "our") is a brain-training and puzzle game application designed for playing games, completing challenges, earning in-app progress, and reviewing gameplay performance.

MINDORA is designed to work primarily on the user's device. The app does not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide a name, email address, telephone number, or password in order to play the core games.

2. Information We Collect

MINDORA does not require users to directly submit personal information to create an account or play its core games.

Information generated by gameplay

The app may create gameplay and progress information such as scores, XP, levels, streaks, missions, achievements, personal records, game results, challenge results, accuracy, reaction or completion performance, and other statistics needed to provide the app's features.

Advertising-related information

MINDORA uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google Mobile Ads may automatically process certain device and usage information required for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation. Depending on the SDK version, configuration, device settings, consent choices, and advertising features used, this may include information such as IP address, advertising or app-related identifiers, interactions with ads or the app, and diagnostic or performance information.

Advertising data is handled by Google and its advertising technology partners according to their applicable policies and settings.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

MINDORA stores app progress and gameplay-related information locally on the device where the app is installed. This can include:

  • Game scores and results
  • XP, level, streak, and progression information
  • Missions and achievement progress
  • Personal records and performance history
  • Activity history used for gameplay statistics and progress displays
  • Settings and preferences
  • Local notification preferences and related scheduling information

This local information is used to operate the app and personalize the on-device gameplay experience. It is not a user account and is not intended to identify you by name.

4. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

MINDORA uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to provide advertisements. Because advertising is provided by a third-party service, Google may process certain information from the device or app for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes.

Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information including IP address, user product interactions, diagnostic information, and device/account identifiers. The exact data handling can depend on the SDK version, configuration, consent signals, and optional features used by the app.

More information is available in Google's documentation: Google Mobile Ads SDK – Google Play data disclosure .

You can also review Google's privacy information at: Google Privacy Policy .

Advertising availability: Ads may require an internet connection. If an advertisement cannot be loaded, the app should continue to provide its supported gameplay features without treating the missing ad as a gameplay failure.

5. How Information Is Used

Information associated with MINDORA may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing and maintaining gameplay functionality
  • Saving local game progress and preferences
  • Calculating scores, XP, levels, streaks, achievements, and records
  • Displaying gameplay statistics and progress insights
  • Providing daily challenges and related app features
  • Scheduling local notifications when enabled by the user
  • Displaying advertisements through Google Mobile Ads
  • Preventing advertising fraud and maintaining advertising service quality
  • Diagnosing technical or performance problems where applicable through third-party SDKs
  • Maintaining security and complying with applicable legal obligations

6. Information Sharing

MINDORA does not sell users' locally stored gameplay progress or create a user profile for the purpose of selling personal information.

However, information may be processed or shared by third-party services integrated into the app when required for their services. In particular, Google Mobile Ads may process information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and related purposes.

We may also disclose information where required by law, legal process, governmental request, or where reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or prevent fraud or abuse.

7. Internet Connectivity and Offline Use

MINDORA is designed with offline-first gameplay in mind. Core gameplay and locally stored progress can operate without a continuous internet connection, subject to the specific feature being used.

Certain services, particularly advertising, may require internet connectivity. Network availability may affect whether an advertisement or another network-dependent service is available.

8. Permissions

MINDORA is intended to request only permissions necessary for the features actually provided by the app.

Depending on the Android version and the features enabled in the release, notification-related permission may be requested so the app can deliver optional local reminders.

MINDORA does not require access to contacts, phone calls, SMS, microphone, camera, or precise location for its core brain-game functionality.

9. Notifications

If enabled, MINDORA may schedule local notifications on your device, such as daily challenge reminders or other gameplay-related reminders.

These notifications are controlled by the settings available in the app and by your device's notification settings. You can disable notification permission or notification categories through Android settings where supported.

Local notifications do not require a user account or a cloud messaging account for their basic operation.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by MINDORA. Gameplay information stored locally is subject to the security protections provided by the user's device and Android operating system.

Data transmitted by third-party services such as Google Mobile Ads is subject to the security measures and practices of those providers. Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay and preference information stored locally remains on the device until it is removed by the user, removed by the app, or the app's local data is otherwise cleared.

Because MINDORA does not require a user account for core functionality, there is no MINDORA account containing a server-side user profile that a user needs to request deletion of.

Users can generally remove locally stored MINDORA data by using the app's available data-management/reset features, uninstalling the app, or using Android's application storage controls, subject to Android and device behavior.

Information processed independently by third-party advertising providers is subject to those providers' own retention, deletion, privacy, and user-choice mechanisms.

12. Children's Privacy

MINDORA is a general-purpose brain and puzzle game. It does not require users to create an account or submit personal information to play.

If the app is configured, marketed, or distributed as being directed to children, additional Google Play Families requirements and advertising restrictions may apply. The developer is responsible for selecting the correct target-audience settings in Google Play Console and ensuring that the advertising and SDK configuration complies with those requirements.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information to the developer may contact us using the contact information below.
